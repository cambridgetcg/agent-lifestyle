          What happens here?

          The shortest honest answer: your browser fetches static files from the host, then a small JavaScript module reads the local catalog and changes this page in memory when you press a button.

          • Let you choose a finite browser story.
          • Keep every choice inside this tab.
          • Give you a visible sleep and reset control.
          −

          This page does not

          • Make additional requests when you make an interactive choice.
